# Product

## Overview

**Ticket to Ride** is a board game, where four players compete to earn points, which are gained by occupying railway
routes and completing objectives. The game board represents the map of Europe, composed of a set of cities connected by
a railway network, whose routes are characterized by a color. During a turn, a player can either draw 2 cards from the
deck or claim a route. A player can claim a route by discarding from its hand a number of cards of the color of the
route equal to the length of the route. Each player has 45 train cars that are placed on the map to occupy the routes,
so when a player has less than 3 train cars left, the last round of the game begins. At the end of the game, the player
with the most points wins.

## Requirements

- The functional requirements are listed in the file `docs/report/requirement_specification.md`;
- a player can be either a human or an AI, following the same rules and logic, so a human player can play against three
AI players.
